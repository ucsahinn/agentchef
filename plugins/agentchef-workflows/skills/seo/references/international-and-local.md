# International and Local SEO

## International

- Choose a stable URL strategy per language or region and keep each version
  crawlable.
- Add reciprocal `hreflang` annotations using supported language and optional
  region codes. Include a self-reference; use `x-default` only when there is a
  genuine default selector or fallback.
- Canonicalize a language page to itself unless it is truly a duplicate of the
  canonical target. Do not collapse translated equivalents into one language.
- Keep language signals consistent across visible content, navigation,
  metadata, canonical URLs, and alternate annotations.
- Avoid forced IP redirects that prevent users and crawlers from choosing or
  discovering locales.

## Local

When a business serves physical locations or service areas:

- verify the real name, address or service area, phone, hours, categories, and
  landing-page relationship;
- keep public business information consistent without creating fake locations;
- use local business structured data only for real visible entities;
- treat Google Business Profile edits, review replies, listing submissions,
  and account changes as external writes requiring explicit approval.

Do not create doorway city pages with interchangeable text. A location page
must offer location-specific usefulness and accurate operational information.

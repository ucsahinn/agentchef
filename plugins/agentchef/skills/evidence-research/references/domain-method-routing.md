# Domain Method Routing

Choose sources and standards that fit the domain.

## Scholarly and biomedical

- Use PubMed, trial registries, Crossref, OpenAlex, publisher pages, and
  discipline databases as appropriate.
- For genuine systematic reviews, use a registered or explicit protocol,
  comprehensive searches, duplicate screening where required, risk-of-bias
  tools, and PRISMA-style reporting.
- Medical conclusions require current clinical guidance and direct evidence;
  they are not a substitute for professional care.

## Legal and policy

- Confirm jurisdiction and effective date.
- Prioritize enacted law, regulations, official guidance, court decisions, and
  primary policy documents.
- Distinguish legal text, agency interpretation, commentary, and practical
  recommendation. Flag the need for qualified legal review.

## Company, market, and competitor

- Prioritize audited filings, regulator databases, official financials,
  investor materials, product documentation, pricing pages, and direct product
  evidence.
- Treat estimates from market-research vendors, traffic tools, employee counts,
  and social profiles as dated estimates with methodology limits.
- Separate announced plans from shipped capabilities and marketing claims from
  observed behavior.

## Technical and standards

- Use official vendor or project documentation, specifications, standards,
  release notes, source repositories, and reproducible tests.
- Check version, platform, and date. Use Context7 or the official docs surface
  for drift-prone library or API behavior.

## SEO

Use `$seo` for technical implementation, rendering, structured data, or
measurement changes. Evidence Research may supply a traceable market,
competitor, audience, or content-opportunity study, but must not claim property
rankings without authorized SEO evidence.

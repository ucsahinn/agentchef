# Verification Rubric

## Charter

- The decision, primary question, sub-questions, audience, scope, exclusions,
  deadline, and deliverables are explicit.
- Assumptions and approval gates are visible.

## Search and screening

- Source classes match the domain and question.
- Queries, filters, dates, and databases are logged at the claimed rigor.
- Duplicates, versions, exclusions, and full-text availability are handled.
- "Comprehensive" or "systematic" is used only when the protocol supports it.

## Evidence and synthesis

- Each source has provenance, checked date, method context, limitations,
  confidence, and outdated risk.
- Every material claim references known source IDs.
- Facts, inferences, and recommendations are distinct.
- Counterevidence, disagreement, applicability, and uncertainty are reported.
- Quotes and source-derived wording stay within legal and platform limits.

## Analysis

- Data provenance, transformations, units, missingness, and exclusions are
  documented.
- Statistical or qualitative methods fit the question and available evidence.
- Code or calculations can be rerun where the deliverable claims
  reproducibility.
- No credentials or restricted raw data appear in outputs.

## Completion

Use `complete` only when all chartered questions and deliverables are satisfied
with no unresolved gaps. Use `partial` when the result is useful but bounded.
Use `blocked` when a required source, authority, or input prevents meaningful
progress. A valid JSON report proves structure and traceability, not truth.

# Measurement and Roadmap

## Measurement design

Tie SEO to a decision and business outcome:

- discovery: valid important URLs, crawl health, submitted versus indexed
  trends;
- visibility: non-brand impressions, query/page coverage, click-through rate,
  and position distributions;
- experience: field LCP, INP, and CLS at origin and URL group level when
  available;
- value: qualified organic conversions, assisted journeys, revenue or another
  agreed outcome;
- quality: content freshness, unsupported-claim defects, and resolved technical
  regressions.

Never define success as raw traffic alone. Document analytics definitions,
consent constraints, attribution limits, and seasonality.

## Prioritization

For each action record:

- affected users, routes, and templates;
- evidence and confidence;
- search eligibility or experience impact;
- implementation owner and dependency;
- effort range stated as a local estimate;
- verification method and rollback;
- external-write or account approval.

Use P0 only for issues that materially block discovery, indexing eligibility,
security, or a critical launch. Rank other work by impact and evidence rather
than copying a universal 30/60/90 schedule.

## Causality

Record deployment dates and compare meaningful pre/post windows, control for
site changes when possible, and state uncertainty. Search changes, competitor
actions, seasonality, tracking changes, and crawl lag can confound outcomes.
Correlation after an SEO change does not by itself prove causation.
